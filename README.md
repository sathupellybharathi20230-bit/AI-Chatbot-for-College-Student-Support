🤖 AI Chatbot for College Student Support

📌 Project Overview

The AI Chatbot for College Student Support is an intelligent chatbot designed to help college students quickly access information and support related to their academic and campus needs.

The chatbot provides students with instant responses to common questions, reducing the need to wait for college staff or search through multiple sources.

🎯 Objectives

- Provide 24/7 support to college students
- Answer frequently asked college-related questions
- Help students find important academic information
- Reduce repetitive work for college staff
- Improve the overall student support experience
- Provide quick and user-friendly responses

✨ Key Features

- 💬 Interactive AI chatbot
- 🎓 Academic information support
- 📅 Information about schedules and events
- 📚 Course and subject-related assistance
- 🏫 Campus information
- 📝 Admission and registration guidance
- 📢 Important announcements and notifications
- ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
- ⚡ Fast responses

🏗️ How It Works

1. The student opens the chatbot.
2. The student enters a question.
3. The chatbot processes the question.
4. The system identifies the user's intent.
5. Relevant information is retrieved.
6. The chatbot generates a response.
7. The response is displayed to the student.

💡 Example Questions

Students can ask questions such as:

- "What are the college timings?"
- "When is the next exam?"
- "How can I apply for admission?"
- "Where is the library?"
- "What documents are required for registration?"
- "When does the semester start?"
- "How can I contact the college office?"
- "What are the available courses?"

2. Open the project folder

3. Install required packages

4. Run the application
